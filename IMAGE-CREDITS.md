The home hero's artwork is in `public/images/hero`. Three pieces come from images supplied by the center: the seated figure, `meditation-silhouette.webp` (from `female-doing-yoga-meditation-mandala-background.webp`, kept beside it and not used by the site), and the pink lotus on the water, `water-lotus.webp` (from an image sent with the brief; stray reflection marks under the flower were removed). The swan on the water, `swan.webp`, is made from a photograph sent with the brief: the bird was lifted from its background, recoloured to a cool white and smoothed to sit with the illustrations. The sources and licences of all three are not recorded here: they look like stock images, so confirm where they came from and whether they need attribution before launch.

Everything else in the hero is drawn in code and needs no credit: the lotus petals, sun and leaves round the figure (`src/components/home/hero-emblem.tsx`), the leaves on the water (`hero-stream.tsx`), and the hills, mist, night sky, lily pads and foreground leaves (`hero-scene.tsx`), styled in `src/app/globals.css`.

The logo in `public/brand` is the center's own artwork, cropped from the supplied brochure.
